# Chris Mahlke

Design engineer in San Mateo, California. I run [M10](https://m10design.ai.studio), an applied R&D studio that does UX research and design engineering for AI systems: studying how people work with models and agents, then building the interfaces.

Before M10 I worked in geographic information systems, which is where the mapping apps below come from.

## Apps

Native apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. No account, no ads, no tracking.

| App | What it does | Links |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opisometer** | Measures distance and area on Apple Maps, along roads and trails or in straight lines, with route recording, elevation profiles and 3D terrain. | [Site](https://chrismahlke.github.io/opisometer/) · [Support](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/opisometer) |
| **Meridian** | The sun, the moon, your heading and the time on one dial, with rises and sets, moon phases and a 3D sky. | [Site](https://chrismahlke.github.io/meridian/) · [Support](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/meridian) |
| **LOC IQ** | The city you are in, on one screen: population, income, housing and commuting from U.S. Census data, beside the cities that border it. | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/loc-iq-city-census-data/id6761205178) · [Site](https://chrismahlke.github.io/lociq/) |
| **Mot Juste** | An offline dictionary of 125,000+ words with three new words a day, spaced review, quizzes and grammar lessons. | [Site](https://chrismahlke.github.io/motjuste/) · [Support](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/motjuste) |
| **Mahjong Four Ways** | A free mahjong game with Hong Kong, Riichi, MCR and American rules. | [Support](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/mahjong) |

## Open source

- [falken](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/falken): offline-first iOS chat that runs quantized language models on device through a Swift wrapper around llama.cpp.
- [terraguard](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/terraguard): offline disaster-response planner that turns incident text into structured forms, with a fine-tuned model. OpenAI hackathon entry.
- [NeuroPulse](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/NeuroPulse): real-time stroke triage and hospital routing on Kafka and Google Cloud AI.
- [doppelganger](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/doppelganger): finds demographically similar U.S. ZIP codes from Census data. Google Cloud Run hackathon entry.
